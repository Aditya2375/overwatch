WHAT THIS VIEW CANNOT TELL YOU

Aircraft cover a 150-nautical-mile circle around Helsinki. Vessel reports cover a 100 km radius. Receiver coverage is incomplete. Reports are not extrapolated; markers stay at their reported coordinates and disappear when stale.

The ISS marker is a source-calculated orbital position, not a direct telemetry measurement. Marker heights are exaggerated for legibility. Aircraft triangles are type symbols, not projected headings. No routes, destinations, worldwide coverage or navigation accuracy are implied.

If a feed fails, its error stays visible. No demo traffic is inserted. Never use this for navigation, safety or operational decisions.
